About

Fairly Lilith is a live band tribute to the women who ruled 90s pop and rock radio — the anthems, the ballads, and the songs that soundtracked a decade of female artists writing their own rules. From Alanis Morissette's raw confessionals to Sarah McLachlan's soaring balladry, from The Cranberries' edge to Sheryl Crow's road-worn songcraft, the band brings the era's most unforgettable female-fronted hits to the stage in a full live set.

Not every artist in the set played the original Lilith Fair — hence the name — but every song belongs in the same conversation: women who defined a sound, a decade, and a generation's relationship to the radio dial. The result is a night built for singing along, loudly and unapologetically, to the songs you never actually forgot the words to.
